## Money is the weakest currency in this market

The thing to understand about political access in India is that ₹12L/yr doesn't register, but it's also not what's being traded. Politicians are short of four things, and cash is the one they have the most reliable supply of:

## The structural work matters more than any relationship

Since this is personal and cross-venture, the real question is how to keep *your name* out of files:

1. **Directorships are the transmission vector.** Personal criminal liability attaches to directors under GST, EPF, s.138 NI Act, labour law and environmental law — largely regardless of involvement. Every board seat you hold is a channel through which a company's trouble becomes your trouble.

3. **Entity separation as a firebreak, not a tax structure.** The point isn't efficiency — it's that a notice in one entity can't reach the others or you.
4. **Personal-guarantee discipline.** A PG converts corporate trouble into personal trouble instantly and is the most common way "invisible" promoters become visible.

## The three ladders

**1. Protocol VIP — closed.** Warrant of precedence, security cover categories, airport protocol, the state-guest list. This attaches to constitutional and statutory office only. Not purchasable, not achievable without holding a post. (Beacons went in 2017 for everyone but emergency services.) Set this aside permanently.

**2. Appointed VIP — the real route, and the one most "connected" promoters actually took.** Government positions conferred by discretion:

- State industrial development corporation boards, state skill development missions, tourism boards, pollution control board advisory committees
- District Industries Centre committees, Regional Transport Authority membership
- University senates and syndicates, boards of governors of NITs/IIITs
- Export Promotion Council boards, commodity boards, Sector Skill Councils under NSDC
- Central: NITI Aayog task forces, ministry advisory committees, BIS technical committees

These come with letterhead, protocol standing, and a genuine phone list. They are handed out by ministers and chief ministers, usually on the recommendation of an industry body — which is why ladder 3 is the actual entry point.

**3. Earned VIP — the industry-body ladder.** Join a chamber → work a sectoral panel for two or three years → convenor → state council office-bearer → state chairman. A CII or FICCI state chairman shares a stage with the Chief Minister as routine business. The arbitrage worth knowing: the climb is dramatically faster in a small sector-specific association than in CII, and the protocol you get in a government room is nearly identical. Pick a pond where a ₹6cr promoter is a big fish.

## What's realistically reachable at each level

| Level | Time to get there | What it actually buys | Your odds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local** (MLA, corporator, district admin) | 18–36 months | Calls returned, thana knows the name, files move, extortion stops | High — genuinely achievable |
| **State** (secretaries, ministers' offices, CM's summit list) | 4–8 years | A secretary takes a meeting; you're on the investor-summit list; route to a minister's OSD | Moderate, with sustained visible effort |
| **National** (Delhi ministries, PMO orbit) | 10+ years or never | Policy-table presence | Effectively closed via this route |

On national: genuine Delhi access tracks capital deployed, employment created, or narrow recognised expertise. At ₹6cr revenue you cannot buy in on the first two. ## The underused shortcuts
