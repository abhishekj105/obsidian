**ASSISTANT:** What you're describing isn't a trading strategy — it's a well-known fraud pattern with three separate offences stacked on top of each other. I'll explain the mechanics honestly, because understanding why it fails matters more than the label.

## What the structure actually is

Break it into its four parts:

**1. Shorting against your own investors' longs.** The trader's short profits *are* the investors' losses, roughly one-for-one. This isn't generating returns from the market — it's transferring money from the pool to the manager. That's trading against clients / front-running.

**2. "Free use of funds."** Investor money deployed for the trader's own book, without that being the disclosed mandate, is misappropriation. The lock-in doesn't create consent; it just removes the exit.

**3. "Manipulate the price up a little more than invested."** This is where the economics collapse. Pushing a stock up requires *buying* — real capital, absorbing real supply. To realise the gain you must *sell* into the same book, which pushes it back down. Net of impact and costs, you end up negative. The only reliable way to fund the "lucrative return" is fresh money from the next investor cohort.

**4. Lock-in + steady above-market payouts.** This is the diagnostic signature. The lock-in exists to delay redemptions; the payouts exist to generate referrals. That combination is what defines a Ponzi rather than a bad fund.

So the scheme doesn't have an edge. It has a *cash-flow illusion* that survives only while inflows exceed payouts, and mathematically that window always closes.

## Legal exposure in India

- **SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003** and **s.12A SEBI Act** — manipulation and fraudulent trading. Penalty is disgorgement plus up to 3x the profit, plus a market ban.
- **Unregistered portfolio management** — pooling third-party money to trade without PMS/AIF registration is independently an offence.
- **BUDS Act, 2019** — accepting deposits with promised returns outside a regulated framework; this one carries imprisonment and lets authorities attach personal assets.
- **BNS cheating / criminal breach of trust** — the criminal track, which runs in parallel and doesn't settle.

The lock-in period is an aggravating fact, not a defence — it's read as evidence the delay was engineered.
